Where the numbers come from. Water levels are real readings from CGWB wells, taken every November. Rain comes from the IMD grid. "How fast the water is dropping" is the long-run trend since 2000.
How the predictions work. A machine learning model learnt how each well responds to rain, using 2000–2017. You pick a monsoon size, and it says where the water would end up. It can never say that more rain leaves less water. The project has a second, simpler model used for a different question.
